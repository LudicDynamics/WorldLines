## In one line

We believe language models and agents are not just for generating stories. They can simulate a world: given laws, an engine and boundary conditions, they can run one. A world simulation can be a game in itself, or all the character agents and the world together can be the foundation a game is built on.

We believe that in such a simulated world, every character is really living there, together with the player. Only a character that lives in a world is a real character.

WorldLines is where you and your AI character play together in a world with no edges. The character has goals, memories and lines it will not cross. The world has its own places, time, rules and consequences. None of it is a script written ahead of time for you.

| What | In one line | Article |

<!-- section: thread -->
## The thread that runs through them

Generative AI gives us endless stories. What we want are stories that never have to end.

In a world that keeps running, people, AI characters and mixes of the two can keep playing for as long as they like. The world moves forward one beat at a time, and everyone submits their actions when it suits them. While you are away, your own agent can play your character, and you take over again when you return. The first thing you will ask is: what happened while I was gone?
