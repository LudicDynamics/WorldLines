## The open part

The NeonRP engine is open source under Apache-2.0 and the WorldLines product under AGPL-3.0, both on [GitHub](https://github.com/LudicDynamics/WorldLines). The specs for worlds, characters, templates, play contracts and rules packs are all public.

The open version runs on your own API key: DeepSeek, any OpenAI-compatible service, or a custom endpoint. Worlds, characters and saves are files on your own machine. The PyPI package plays the full two-beat turn; a source install plays a single beat. See [Connect a model](/docs/players/providers).

<!-- section: platform -->
## The platform part

[worldlines.gg](https://worldlines.gg) and the iOS app are for everyone else. There is nothing to install and no key to configure: pick a world on the Hub and play.

An account adds what the local version does not have: the Jev judge, which decides in under a second whether the planning talk is settled; the official worlds; publishing the worlds you make; and sharing the games you have played.

<!-- section: one -->
## Both sides run the same engine

The local version and the platform run the same engine and the same specs. A world you build locally can be published to the Hub and played on the web and on phones, and a character carries its memories back and forth between the two.

We do not want to lock the engine inside the platform. People who can run it themselves do. People who want it easy use the platform. Both are playing the same kind of world.

<!-- section: community -->
## Community

Worlds, characters and orchestration should not come only from us. The open engine and the public specs exist so that the community can provide them.

Questions, bug reports, worlds of your own and changes to the code are all welcome on [Discord](https://discord.gg/HJYWbdqWrE) and GitHub.
