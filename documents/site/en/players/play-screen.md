## Start a run

> **Status: implemented · not yet released**

Pick a world on the overview and start a run; you land on the **test bench**: the conversation on the left, your state and the characters present on the right. For a complete short run, try **Greyoak House** (percentile investigation): the client's uncle walked into this house three days ago and never came out — find out why in a handful of turns.

<!-- section: beats -->
## Two beats: plan, then resolve

Every turn has two parts, in two colors:

| Beat | Color | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan** | green | the host sets the scene and hands the word to whoever should speak; you and the characters agree what to do and whether to roll. The world has not changed yet |
| **Resolve** | red | once it is agreed, the host writes what happens, then the result lands on the world: moves, wounds, items, clues … |

Say "let's do it" (or similar) and planning moves on to resolve; `@dm` asks the host to carry on.

<!-- section: read -->
## What you see in the conversation

- **Streaming prose**: the host and each character get their own blocks, so who is talking is clear.
- **Dice cards**: one per check — skill, target, roll, outcome (critical / success / failure / fumble).
- **State changes**: wounds, sanity loss, items, new places … as small chips under the beat.
- **Settling**: after the prose, the engine still checks, writes and lets characters react — a few seconds of "settling", not a hang.
- **Your conditions**: "You" on the right — health, sanity, conditions (e.g. poisoned, 2 turns left).
- **The ending**: reach an ending and you get a card with its CG: the ending's name, the truth, what this run gave you.

<!-- section: control -->
## Controls

- **Stop**: while a turn runs, send turns into "■ Stop" — the engine stops after the current step.
- **Scroll up**: reading back no longer snaps you to the bottom; "↓ Latest" brings you back.
- **Right panel**: "Player" shows your state, "Characters" what the people present are thinking.
- **Go back to a turn**: every run is a world line; branch from any turn, see [Saves · branches · undo](/docs/players/saves).
