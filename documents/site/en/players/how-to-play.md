## Talking is acting

> **Status: mixed** — each section says what ships with 1.0 and what is still in design.

Say what you want to do in plain language. Every line is a proposal: the world agent writes the narration, and the world's changes are listed below it (e.g. `player.gold 18 → 16`); anything that can't happen comes with the reason.

<!-- section: move -->
## Moving

"Go to the adventurers' guild", "go to Jadestone". Within a town you walk straight there; between towns you first reach the gate and then take the road, which takes time. Stoneford → Jadestone is a 45-li post road where the One-Eyed Tiger's men are said to lurk.

<!-- section: map -->
## The map

The map shows the places you know and who is where.

> In design (3.0): a map-first play view — click a place to enter its scene and conversation; on the road it shows your trip's progress, and new places may appear along it.

<!-- section: souls -->
## Talking to protagonists

Just address them: "Elena, wait for me here", "Rowan, come with us". Protagonists answer by their own judgement and may not do what you say. Only protagonists who are **present** can answer.

<!-- section: wait -->
## Waiting · auto-advance

"Wait a while" moves time forward; protagonists elsewhere keep acting on their goals.

> In design (3.0): with auto-advance on, the world moves by itself tick by tick — caravans depart, factions act, rumors spread, even when you say nothing.
