## Pick the world

> **Status: ships with 1.0**

On the local home page, start a new game of **Stoneford · Elena & Rowan**. You wake at the north gate in heavy fog, remembering nothing.

- **Elena** — an amnesiac healer; she meets you at the north gate and follows you;
- **Rowan** — an adventurer in Jadestone chasing a legend at the edge of the world; after 11:00 he sets out on his own;
- **Helun** (the guild), **Mira** (the inn) — NPCs driven by the world.

<!-- section: try -->
## Try these first

```text
look around
Elena, do you know someone called Rowan?
go to the adventurers' guild
I'll take a contract
```

If you ask about Rowan while he isn't there, the world tells you "he is not here" — not a bug: each protagonist has their own place in the world.
