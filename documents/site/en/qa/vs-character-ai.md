## What Character AI does well

- Frictionless onboarding — one-click to chat with a character
- Huge community library of character cards
- Mobile-native

<!-- section: where-worldlines-differs -->
## Where WorldLines is different

Two differences matter most:

1. **World-centric, not character-centric.** Character AI gives you a character to chat with. WorldLines builds every AI persona **inside a living world** — characters exist in a place, surrounded by events, other agents, and consequences, not as a chat in a vacuum.

2. **Persistent personas + simulation, not conversation.** A character's personality, memory, and relationships are **persisted** — they carry across sessions, and even across worlds (the same Elena can travel from Stoneford to Kagura Island). And the engine runs a **simulation**, not a dialogue: agents act on their own, the world moves with or without you, and what happens is recorded as state you can save, branch, and replay.

Concretely: persistent world + character state, multi-agent orchestration, file-backed / git-versioned worlds, local-first models (GLM / LM Studio / Ollama), and reviewable Plan → Diff → Apply changes.

<!-- section: who-should-use-which -->
## Who should use which

- Casual chat with a favorite character → Character AI
- Building or playing a world you want to keep, fork, and evolve → WorldLines
