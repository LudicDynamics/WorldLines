## The Agentic Harness

WorldLines is built on an **agentic harness** that connects **world-agents**, **place-agents**, and **soul-agents** into one living simulation. Every component is an agent. The human joins through an avatar terminal. This is a **multi-agent society** — each agent has its own goals, memory, and constraints.

<!-- section: emergent -->
## Orchestration → a living world

On top of that harness, we use **orchestration** to run a full **world simulation** — and let drama *emerge* from it. Story isn't scripted; it surfaces from the friction between you and the **soul-agents** as they pursue their own goals. The orchestrator keeps the world coherent; the souls make it alive and unpredictable.

<!-- section: social-experiments -->
## Beyond SNS — worlds for social experiments

Multi-agent simulation isn't only for social networks. We want WorldLines to host **social experiments in worlds beyond SNS** — towns, courts, expeditions, fantasy societies — where many agents act under real constraints and you can watch what emerges.

<!-- section: core -->
## The core — make the souls come alive

But the heart of it is simple. Above every benchmark and experiment, our central goal keeps coming back to one thing: making **every soul-agent genuinely come alive** inside the simulation — to remember, to want, to change, to surprise you. A world is only as alive as the people living in it.

<!-- section: use-cases -->
## What we built this engine for

- interactive-experience creation
- multi-agent society experiments (beyond social networks)
- agent-research advancement
- personality-model and world-model benchmarks

The harness makes these possible by giving creators **constraints, not scripts**.
