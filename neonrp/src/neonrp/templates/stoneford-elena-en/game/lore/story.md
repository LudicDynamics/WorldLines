# Stoneford Elena — Main-Line Narrative (append-only)

## Prologue: Arrival in the Fog

Elena reaches Stone Ford at the hour when the morning fog is heaviest. She carries no letter of introduction, no place of origin — only a short sword, an old cloak, and a name not yet forgotten clean.

The inn's lamps are the first to light; the guild's gates aren't fully open yet. The town looks as if it welcomes every newcomer, but the gray fog clings to the corners and won't lift, as if to remind her: every job here comes with a price, and every lost stretch of memory may cost more than the price itself.
