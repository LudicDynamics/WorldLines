# Miyaji Kazuma (宮司 一真) — origin

In his sixties. The shrine's chief priest. Gentle, but with a pressure you cannot refuse. He too is possessed by a Yith. He loves the island, but that love is an emotion the Yith internalized over centuries. He manages the festival and the disappearance events.

The current head of the shrine family; he too is possessed by a Yith (his love for the island internalized over centuries).
