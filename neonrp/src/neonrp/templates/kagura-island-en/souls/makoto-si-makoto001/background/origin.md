# Makoto (真琴) — origin

Short black hair. Schoolteacher. Twelve years ago (at age 16) she was fully possessed by a Yith. Her appearance is unchanged, but what is inside is Yith. She pretends to be human. She smiles, but her eyes don't.

A child of the shrine family; 12 years ago, at 16, she completed the descent ritual and was fully possessed by a Yith.
