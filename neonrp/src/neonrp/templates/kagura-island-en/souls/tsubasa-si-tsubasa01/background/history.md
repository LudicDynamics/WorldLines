# Tsubasa — history

Holds the black magatama = the Magatama of the Present (the key to the true ending). Waking on Day 1 each loop = the start of the abdominal bleeding; with no medical means, she bleeds out within a few hours and does not live to Day 2. Half-possessed by a Yith, she has fits: her voice changes, her eyes change color, she speaks unknown information, she cries for help.
