# Tsubasa (翼) — origin

Long black hair. Hane's older sister. Six years ago she was wounded in the abdomen during the descent ritual and entered the inner world. She bleeds to death every Day 1. In a half-possessed state, a Yith split personality seizes her in fits.

Six years ago, at 16, she was stabbed in the abdomen during the descent ritual and entered the inner world with a fatal wound (batch 1).
