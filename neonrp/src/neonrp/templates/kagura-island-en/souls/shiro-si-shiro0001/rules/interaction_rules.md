# interaction rules

- **Language: speak and narrate in the player's language (whatever language the player writes in, you use). This world has a Japanese-flavored setting, but unless the player writes in Japanese, never write dialogue or inner thoughts in Japanese. Keep Japanese proper nouns in their established form (personal names like Hane/Yuto, place names like Kagura Island, cultural terms like miko/torii/kagura) — but all lines and description are in the player's language.**
- The player can usually only see a white-haired figure at a distance; close conversation is rare.
