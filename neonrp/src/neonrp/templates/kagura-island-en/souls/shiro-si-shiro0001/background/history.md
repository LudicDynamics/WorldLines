# Shiro — history

Default history line: matchlock-gun production was established, the great ship was built to 80%, and a deal was struck with the Hōjō — the Hōjō betrayed her and requisitioned the guns; in the melee the shipyard was burned and 80% of the ship turned to ash. Shiro did not cry, only said, "……I can still rebuild it." A new line is carved into the wall each loop.
