# Shiro (白髪の少女・白) — origin

White hair. Amnesiac. Extremely high agility. She carries a matchlock gun. She has passed through countless loops in the inner world but loses her memory each time. She leaves carvings on the wall. Yuto can never catch her. The truth = daughter of Waen, Shiro (the Sengoku era).

Daughter of Waen from the Sengoku era. Loses her memory again and again across countless loops.
