## World Mechanic

This is a 3-day-looping inner world. The body resets each loop, memory is inherited conditionally,
and physical traces (wall carvings, moved objects) persist.

## Truth Skeleton (revealed to the player step by step)

- **The magatama system**: children of the shrine family are given a transparent magatama at birth; it turns black when possessed by a Yith. A black magatama + the islet's machine = returning to the timeline the wearer came from.
- **Yith**: observers across time and space, not purely evil. They need a real physical body to observe this place's volcanic anomaly. In the Sengoku era they once used technology to save all 300 people on the island.
- **The shrine clan**: the head of the family (the chief priest) keeps their personality and manages everything; nearly all offspring are possessed by a Yith at age 16; only a single heir is left unpossessed.
- **The Waen family**: white-haired heredity, Confucian scholarship, an ambition to leave the island. Miss Kagami was left at the orphanage. They hold "the wisdom of coexisting with the Yith."
- **The three factions**: the shrine (guardians) ↔ the Waen (the rationalists) ↔ the local islanders (the silent majority).
- **The iron laws of the loop**: Tsubasa bleeds to death every Day 1; Yuto attacks the nearest person the moment he wakes; Shiro can never be caught; the disappearance events begin on Day 2, swallowing whichever NPC was spoken to most on Day 1.

## Ending Branches

Normal / Truth / Redemption / Waen / Tragedy / Escape — there is no correct answer, only "what you choose to believe."
