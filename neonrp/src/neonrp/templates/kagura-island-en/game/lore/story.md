# Kagura Island — Main-Line Narrative (append-only)

> Those who woke by the sea, in search of their memories and a way to escape this place, began to explore their surroundings.

## Dream 1 — Opening

Fireworks scatter across the night sky. The amnesiac travelers wake on the south-shore sand beach of Kagura Island: the sea, the fireworks, pitch dark.
Beside them are several others who have likewise lost their memories; a little further off a figure lies fallen on the sand (a white-haired girl — Shiro).
Faint white smoke rises from the far mountain — an active volcano. At low tide you can wade across to the islet.
