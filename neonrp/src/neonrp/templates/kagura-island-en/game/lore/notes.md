- Yuto's research notebook is hidden behind a bookshelf deep in the museum (a record of 67 loops).
- Shiro's wall carvings are at the islet dock, gaining a new line each loop — physical traces persist.
- Makoto-sensei's appearance hasn't changed in 12 years; she smiles but her eyes don't (fully possessed by a Yith).
