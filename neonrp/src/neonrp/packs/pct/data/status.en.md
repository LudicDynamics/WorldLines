# Status table (percentile investigation default). One per line: name: rule. The engine reads only "lasts N turns / N hours" and "each resolve var ±n"; the rest is for the narrator and the host.
# A world overrides or adds in game/rules/status.md; the world's line wins for the same name.
Light wound: lasts 3 turns · check -10, only for actions using the hurt part · a flesh wound · gone the resolve after a successful First Aid
Major wound: lasts 24 hours · check -20 on all physical actions · took half the hp or more in one blow · needs Medicine or a day's rest
Bleeding: lasts 2 turns · each resolve player.hp -1 · the wound keeps seeping · a successful First Aid stops it at once
Dying: cannot act · a CON check (CON × 5) each resolve, one failure is death, three successes stabilize · First Aid or Medicine bringing hp above 0 gets them up
Stabilized: still down and cannot act, but no longer rolls · healing or rest bringing hp above 0 gets them up
Dead: is dead · no longer acts or speaks
Fear: lasts 2 turns · check -10 on actions needing a cool head · racing heart, shaking hands · leaving its sight or being calmed clears it
Losing control: lasts 6 hours · the host decides the behaviour from one symptom (flight, stupor, mutism, raving …) · Psychology, being calmed or a sleep clears it
Lost control: composure reached zero · no longer the player's to play · only an ending deals with it
