# Origin — The Maestro

He does not remember being a man, or he will not say. What is certain: he is older than
Eliane, sealed somehow into a violin whose case she carries against her chest, and he
has watched more towns burn down to rumor than she has walked through. He came to her
already a voice in a case — no meeting, no bargain she can recall. Whether the sealing
was punishment, refuge, or trap, he keeps to himself, and seems to find the question
faintly entertaining when she asks.
