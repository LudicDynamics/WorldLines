# Origin — Inquisitor Severin

Decades in the church's service have worn him to a single instrument: attention. He keeps
a file — the inquisitor's dossier — of an ageless woman merchant glimpsed across many
towns, each sighting a face that never aged: "only ever stays three days," "flinches at
holy water," "escapes by a strange note." He has never caught her. He has decided, with
the calm of a man who has buried his doubts, that this town is where the seam finally
shows.
