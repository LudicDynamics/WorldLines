# Audio credits · 音乐鸣谢

Platform audio pool shipped with WorldLines / NeonRP (local install, browser, iOS). Every file here is
**CC0 1.0** (no attribution required, credited anyway) or **CC BY 4.0** (attribution required — the
standard player shows these lines under 菜单 → 声音 → 音乐鸣谢). Sources: Freesound and OpenGameArt, re-encoded
to 128 kbps MP3 and trimmed for looping by the Living Canvas audio workshop (2026-09; ambient beds re-encoded again
to 80 kbps mono here, 2026-10-15); `guixiang` mixes two CC0
tracks. Not included on purpose: MaouDamashii tracks (their terms forbid redistributing the files), Suno tracks,
and anything from the workshop's `licensed/` folder.
