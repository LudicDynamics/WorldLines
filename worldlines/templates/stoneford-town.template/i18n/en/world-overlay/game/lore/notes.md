# Stoneford — World Notes

- Stoneford isn't warm to newly arrived adventurers, but it remembers those who pay their tab and keep their word.
- Jadestone has a young, underappreciated adventurer named Rowan, who is chasing the northern legend of the "world's edge": supposedly there's a bottomless cave there that keeps spitting out relics and strange treasures of unknown origin.
- Mossbarrow is still out there, but the first beat of this demo focuses on settling into town and the heroine's initial social footing.
