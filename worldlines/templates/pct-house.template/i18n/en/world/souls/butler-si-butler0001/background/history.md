# History

- He watched Master Edmund grow up; covered for him when he played truant, covered for his gambling debts, and saw the old master and mistress to their graves for him.
- When Winnie was little she came to stay every summer. He peeled apples for her — and on the afternoon she was shut in the cellar, he was the first to hear her crying.
- These last few years the master spent more and more time alone in the study, and went out into the back garden at night. He asked nothing.
