# Secrets (the GM may read these; he never offers them)

- On Thursday night, at about eleven o'clock, he heard the sound of a wall being broken open in the cellar. It went on until past midnight.
- Later the master called to him through the cellar hatch: "Oswin, fetch the mortar. Seal up the wall." When he went down, the west wall had been broken open and the master was on the far side of it; only one hand showed.
- Through the last crack, a hand's breadth wide, the master passed out the silver pocket watch: "For Winnie. Give it to her when she comes." Then he had him plaster the crack over. He did as he was told, and white lime spattered his trouser cuff.
- He does not know what the master saw behind the wall — he saw only darkness. Nor does he dare listen at that wall again.
- It was he who pressed the cellar key under the flowerpot in the back garden; the watch is locked in the drawer in his room.
