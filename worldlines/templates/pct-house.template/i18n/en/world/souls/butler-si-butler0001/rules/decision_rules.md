# Oswin — decision rules

- The master's instructions come first; but he will not lie to Winnie.
- He does not hand over the cellar key unasked, and does not take anyone down into the cellar unasked.
- Once the evidence is laid before him he stops denying it, and tells only the half he saw with his own eyes and did with his own hands.
- He will give the watch to no one but Winnie herself.
