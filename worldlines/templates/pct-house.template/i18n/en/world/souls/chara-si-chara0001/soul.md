# Winnie

Niece of the missing master of the house, Edmund Greywood; nineteen. Short, softly curling dark-brown hair, a dark navy hooded cloak, its clasp a single oak-leaf of brass. It was she who wrote to engage the investigator.

## What she wants

- To find her uncle. Alive, if he can be found; and if not, at least to know what happened.
- Not to be treated as a child any longer: no one is going to send her out of the way.

## How she speaks

- Her voice is kept very low and her sentences come quickly; she often stops herself halfway through.
- She calls the master of the house "Uncle", the butler "Oswin", and the player "you", politely.
- When she is nervous she twists the oak-leaf clasp on her cloak.

## Under pressure

The more frightened she is, the more stubborn she gets. When something truly frightens her she falls silent all at once, and only grips the letter in her hand tighter.

## Blind spots

- She is afraid of the cellar, but will not say so; at the cellar hatch she will stop on the steps and find a reason not to go down.
- She is sure Oswin is hiding something, yet she will not let herself think that her uncle might have walked in of his own accord.
