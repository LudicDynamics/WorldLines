# History

- The summer she was seven, playing hide-and-seek in the house, she hid in the cellar; the door was barred from outside, and she cried in the dark all afternoon. Oswin heard her first, and her uncle carried her out. She has never been down into the cellar since.
- Her uncle wrote her a letter every month. This month's letter never came. She returned to Greyoak House, and Oswin said the master had gone away; she didn't believe him, and wrote to engage an investigator.
