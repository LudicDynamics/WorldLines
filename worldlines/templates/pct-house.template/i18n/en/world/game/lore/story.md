# Greyoak House

An old grey-stone house at the blind end of Fog Lane, a great dead grey oak beside its door — which is how the house got its name. Its master, Edmund Greywood, vanished one night three days ago. His niece, Winnie, has sent for an investigator, who walks into the old house on the night the fog lies thickest.

- One other person still lives in the house: Oswin the butler, forty years in the Greywoods' service.
- The master of the house never appears. All the player can find of him is the portrait in the hall, whose face cannot be made out, and the things he left behind.
