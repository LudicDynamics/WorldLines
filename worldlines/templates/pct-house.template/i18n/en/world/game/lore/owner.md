# The master of the house · Edmund Greywood (never appears)

Fifty-two, master of Greyoak House, Winnie's uncle. Never married; he loved his books, the old grey oak, and the niece who came to stay for a month every summer. These last few years he spent more and more time alone in the study, and went out into the back garden at night.

- **He never appears**: he does not speak, is not seen and is not found. Behind the wall there is only ever a black crack.
- What he left in the house: the half-length portrait in the hall (the varnish darkened, the face unreadable; only the watch chain in his hand can be made out), the half-burnt diary in the study grate, the empty watch stand by his bed and an old photograph, and the stopped silver pocket watch.
- When people speak of him, they speak of what he did, not of how he looked: a letter to Winnie every month, teaching her the names of the trees, carrying her out of the cellar the summer she was seven.
