# World notes

- One night of mystery horror: the dread comes only from "something new, clean and freshly disturbed turning up in an old place". No blood, no bodies, no monsters.
- Period: the early twentieth century, on the edge of an unnamed old English town. No telephones; no motor car comes down Fog Lane.
- This world calls mental wear "Nerve"; losing control comes in two kinds, "Losing grip" and "Gone under".
