# Rules (percentile-dice investigation)

- Checks: d100 ≤ skill value succeeds; 01–05 is a critical, 96–00 a fumble; characteristic checks use the characteristic × 5.
- Opposed: both sides roll; the higher grade of success wins, and on the same grade the larger margin wins.
- Combat: attack with d100 ≤ fighting skill; the defender dodges or parries; damage = weapon die + damage bonus; losing half or more of your hit points in one blow is a major wound.
- HP at 0: dying; at each resolution make a Constitution check, and on a failure you die; First Aid that brings HP back to 1 or more gets you on your feet.
- Nerve: on seeing what should not be seen, [san success loss/failure loss]; losing 5 or more in one go may mean Losing grip, and Nerve at zero means Gone under.
